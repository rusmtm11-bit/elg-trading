An international B2B trading company

Executive Level Global Trading LLC is a trading company based in Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates, specialising in the supply of automotive spare parts, tyres and mechanical and engineering equipment.

Who we are

Focused on the parts that matter.

We help businesses keep vehicles, machines and equipment in service by supplying the components, consumables and tyres they need — reliably, accurately and with clear documentation.

Our work is built around a simple idea: a trading partner should save its customers time. That means understanding part numbers and specifications, offering realistic options, confirming lead times honestly and following each order through to delivery.

Licensed activities

What we are
licensed to trade.

Our product range is defined by the activities in our trade licence.

  • ME-01
    Mechanical & Engineering Equipment Trading

    Mechanical and engineering equipment, spare parts and components for engines, motors, pumps, gearboxes, steering systems and starters; pipes and hoses for hydraulic and gas systems, belts, bearings, gears, crankshafts, pistons and other components.

  • AP-02
    Auto Spare Parts Trading

    Spare parts and components for the repair and maintenance of cars, buses, trucks and trailers, including oils, cleaning products, paints and related goods.

  • TY-03
    Tyres & Related Products Trading

    Different types of tyres and related products, including rims, inner tubes and other components.
